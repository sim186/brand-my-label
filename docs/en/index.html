FAQ

Do you store my labels?

No. The label is processed in memory and discarded right away: never written to disk or logged. Your logo stays in your browser only.

How much does it cost?

It's free. If it saves you time you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi, but there's no obligation.

What if there's no room for the logo?

You get the label back unchanged and we tell you. We never cover barcodes or addresses.

Which logo works best?

A PNG with a transparent background, black-and-white or dark: label printers are often monochrome.

Can I hook it up to my printer?

Yes: the code is open source on GitHub, including a command-line version you can wire to your printer.

Your courier is missing or the logo lands in the wrong place?

Let me know: open an issue on GitHub (there's a form for it). Or email me at . GitHub issues are public: black out names and addresses before attaching a screenshot, or send the PDF by email.
